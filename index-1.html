Why we're here

The familiar is a beginning, not a boundary.

We don't have to reject tradition or the well-known. We can respect what works and still stay curious about what else might be possible.

01—∞

There is a difference between choosing a path because it feels right and choosing it simply because it is the only path we've been shown.

Beyond the Conventional is an independent ideas project about curiosity, creativity and independent thinking. It brings together thought-provoking questions, surprising discoveries, original writing and small experiments that invite us to see things from another angle.

“You don't have to know the answer to ask a question worth exploring.”

Our aim isn't to tell anyone what to think. It's to make room for more thoughtful questions—and let people decide where those questions take them.

A tiny discovery

One problem. Many possible answers.

?

Designers, scientists, writers and inventors often make progress by reframing a problem—not only by working harder on the first solution.

Try this: pick an everyday object, such as a paper clip or an empty box. List ten possible uses for it. The first few may be obvious; the later ones ask you to stretch your imagination.

The point isn't to find the “best” answer. It's to notice how many answers become visible when you keep looking.

A prompt, not a test. There are no marks.

The people behind the project

A project born in Srinagar.

Local beginnings. Open horizons.

Beyond the Conventional is an independent ideas project created by Aayat Arif and Ruwa Zehra from Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir, India. It began with a simple belief: there is more to discover than what we are accustomed to seeing.

A

Aayat Arif

Co-creator

Helping shape a space for curiosity, independent thinking and ideas that invite people to look again.

R

Ruwa Zehra

Co-creator

Building a project that makes room for fresh perspectives, original expression and possibilities beyond familiar boundaries.

This project is not about rejecting every convention. It is about asking thoughtful questions, listening to different perspectives and making space for ideas that might otherwise go unexplored.

Before you go

The world is wider than the first answer.

There will always be more questions to ask, perspectives to consider and possibilities to discover. You don't have to find one perfect alternative. You only have to stay curious enough to look.

Keep questioning. Keep discovering. Look beyond.
